Seekwell Locations - data credits
=================================

Data.lua (mob zones and spawn points) is derived from the pfQuest unit / zone database
(shagu, https://github.com/shagu/pfQuest), which is itself based on the CMaNGOS classic-db
project (https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db). Both are GPL-3.0. It reached this addon via
the author's earlier addon ForeverLootBrowser (Data/NpcLoc.lua), converted by a build script
(coords rounded to 3 decimals, at most 40 points per npc, one zone per npc).

Licence: GPL-3.0, see LICENSE.txt (https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html).

Scope: the data covers ORIGINAL Classic (vanilla 1.12) spawns only. Mobs, spawns or zones that
are new or moved on Forever are not in it, and points are approximate.

This folder is optional. Deleting Seekwell_Locations does not affect Seekwell: map pins then
fall back to positions learned from your own loot.
